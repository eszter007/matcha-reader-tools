🍵 Matcha Reader Tools

Manga Dictionary Fonts Furigana & Pinyin

Prepare content for your Matcha Reader

Tools for reading Japanese and Chinese on the reader. Everything runs in your browser and your files never leave your device, except where you choose Google's Gemini under your own API key: manga panel images for text recognition, or a book's text for AI furigana and pinyin.

📖

Manga Converter

Turn a CBZ, EPUB, PDF, or a folder of page images into the reader's manga format — manga, manhua and comics alike, with panel detection, text recognition of the speech bubbles, and translations into your language.
📚

Dictionary Converter

Convert Japanese dictionaries (Jitendex, JMnedict, grammar references, jmdict-simplified, MDict) or Chinese and Cantonese ones (CC-CEDICT, the MoE dictionary, CC-Canto, with frequency ranking, HSK/TOCFL tags and example sentences) into the on-device lookup format.
🈷

Furigana & Pinyin

Add furigana to a Japanese EPUB, or pinyin or zhuyin to a Chinese one, shown above the text on the reader and switched off with a toggle. From the dictionary, or read in context by AI.
🔤

Font Converter

Convert TTF/OTF fonts (Japanese or Chinese reading fonts, for instance) into .cpfont files for the SD card, with kerning and ligatures.

Getting files onto the device

Each tool downloads a zip laid out for the SD card. Either unzip it onto the card with a card reader, or use the reader's built-in Wi-Fi web file transfer to upload the files.

Companion tools for the Matcha Reader firmware (a CrossPoint Reader fork with Japanese and Chinese learning features). Output formats match the firmware's Python conversion tools byte-for-byte. Open source, MIT licensed.
