Manga Converter

Turns a manga, manhua or comic into a book you read panel by panel on your Matcha Reader. Everything runs in your browser; your pages are not uploaded anywhere.

1. Choose your manga

One book, or several at once for a whole series.

How pages are ordered

By file name, cover and copyright first. A .cbz or .zip with a folder per chapter gets one chapter per folder (or the chapters its ComicInfo.xml marks). EPUBs use their spine order and table of contents. PDFs use document order, rasterized at 2×.

    4. Text recognition (Gemini)

    The language translations come back in. Pick the book's own language to get the text without a translation. The language the book is written in comes from Language in Book details. Set it: without it the model guesses, and can read a German speech bubble as Japanese.

    Each panel image goes to Gemini. A whole volume takes a while, so keep this tab open; the screen stays awake, and rate limits are retried automatically.

    Install on the device

    Copy the .matcha file anywhere on the SD card, by card reader or over the reader's Wi-Fi file transfer. /manga/ is a nice convention. A .matcha is a normal zip: rename it to .zip to unpack it on your computer.

    The legacy download is a zip holding one folder, named after the title. Unzip it onto the card; the device finds any folder containing panels.idx, at any depth.
