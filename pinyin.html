🈷 Pinyin Ruby

Adds pinyin (or zhuyin) above the text of a Chinese EPUB, the way Japanese books carry furigana. The reader shows it from the book itself and its Furigana toggle hides it. Every run of characters is split into CC-CEDICT words by longest match, and each character gets its syllable.

2. Options

Reading it

Copy the annotated EPUB to the card like any other book. The readings appear as ruby above each word; Reader Settings → Furigana switches them off and on per book. Existing ruby, scripts, styles and the document head are left untouched.

Output matches tools/pinyin_ruby/add_pinyin_ruby.py from the matcha-reader firmware.
