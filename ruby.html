🈷 Furigana & Pinyin

Adds readings above the text of an EPUB: furigana over the kanji of a Japanese book, pinyin or zhuyin over the characters of a Chinese one. The reader shows them from the book itself, and its Furigana or Pinyin / Zhuyin toggle hides them. Readings the book already carries are kept.

2. Where the readings come from

AI sends the whole text of the book to Google's Gemini API, sentence by sentence, under your own key. Pictures and the file itself stay here. A novel takes a few hundred requests, so the free tier may slow it down; the page retries and keeps the screen awake.

3. Chinese options

Dictionary or AI?

Chinese, dictionary: each character takes the reading of the dictionary word it belongs to. Most are right, but a character with several readings standing alone takes its first one: 石 comes out as dàn rather than shí, 說 as shuì rather than shuō. Chinese, AI: Gemini reads each sentence and picks the reading meant there, checked against CC-CEDICT.

Japanese is AI only. A kanji's reading depends on the word and the sentence (今日 is きょう or こんにち, 行った is いった or おこなった), which a word list cannot settle. A reading is added only when it is kana and fits the word as written, so okurigana stays bare and anything doubtful is left without furigana rather than guessed.

Output matches tools/furigana_ruby/add_furigana_ruby.py and tools/pinyin_ruby/add_pinyin_ruby.py from the matcha-reader firmware.
