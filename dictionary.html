Which dictionary is this?

Put it on the reader

Unzip the download at the root of the SD card, or upload it with the reader's Wi-Fi file transfer. The files land in /dict/ and Word Lookup finds them on its own.

What goes where

Each language has its own folder: /dict/ (or /dictionaries/jp/) for Japanese, /dictionaries/zh/ for Chinese, /dictionaries/yue/ for books tagged Cantonese. A folder holds up to three dictionaries:

vocab.idx    vocab.dat    vocab.spx    vocab.title      (vocabulary — required)
names.idx    names.dat    names.spx    names.title      (names — optional)
grammar.idx  grammar.dat  grammar.spx  grammar.title    (grammar — optional)

A Chinese dictionary built with proper nouns split off writes names.* beside vocab.* in one go. Large dictionaries (Jitendex is about 60 MB) take a minute and some memory to convert.

Output matches tools/dict_convert/convert_jmdict.py and scripts/gen_dict_spx.py from the matcha-reader firmware byte-for-byte. The built-in Chinese sources and their licences are listed in data/README.md.
