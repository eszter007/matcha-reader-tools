Google Gemini (optional, with your own API key)

Some features can send data to Google's Gemini API: text recognition and translation in the Manga converter, and AI readings in Furigana & Pinyin. They are used only when you enter your own Gemini API key and start such a conversion. Your browser then sends the data directly to Google (generativelanguage.googleapis.com); it does not pass through me, and I do not receive or store it.

What is sent: for manga, the images of the panels; for furigana and pinyin, the text of the book, sentence by sentence; in both cases your API key and the technical data of the connection, including your IP address. A book or manga may contain personal data of others, for example in a memoir or letters; please keep this in mind.

Who processes it: Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA, under the terms you agreed to when creating your API key. When you use the free tier of the Gemini API, Google may use what you send and the responses to improve its products, and human reviewers may read them; with a paid API account Google states that it does not. See the Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy. Google LLC participates in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis: your consent, given by entering your key and starting the conversion (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future by not using these features and deleting the key from the form or your browser's site data.
